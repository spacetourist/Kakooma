# Kakooma v0.3.8

Maths trainer for Greg Tang's KAKOOMA puzzle. Pick a level (Easy 10, Medium 20, Hard 50: the biggest number on the board, the sum included) and a board (square, pentagon, hexagon or heptagon: 4–7 numbers; Easy offers 4 or 5, because 6 or 7 different numbers up to 10 can't make a fair board). Exactly one number is the sum of two others; tap it. 60-second rounds, streaks (Blaze grows at x5, x10 and x20), a 3-second penalty for a wrong tap, and a leaderboard per board.

Play: https://spacetourist.github.io/Kakooma/

- Correct answer and board reset (v0.3.8, GD + AE): no per-solve confetti or streak showers any more, in either Show counting mode (the lesson's caption burst is gone too); the only confetti left is the new-#1 shower on the results screen. A correct pick: the answer pops green 1 → 1.12 → 1 over 180 ms, a white tick badge pops onto its top-right corner (no sound), one soft green ring grows 1x → 1.4x while fading over 300 ms, the bond lines still draw and the score goes up by 1 (wrong taps unchanged). Blaze hops on every solve (8 px, 240 ms, squash on landing); his poses are kept for special moments (cheer at a streak of 3/5/10, clap at the lesson's flash at 10, whoa for a fast solve; oops, think and the comeback thumbs-up unchanged). The ordinary-solve sound is the `hop()` hook (sound pack v04: three soft landing plops, rotated by the pack, never the same twice, landing with the squash); it is skipped when Blaze plays a pose sound, and streaks keep the cheer. Then the board resets in ~700 ms (both modes, after the beat or the lesson): collapse 280 ms (ease-in, every tile shrinks to 0.2x at the centre, 20 ms stagger; `collapse()` fires on its first frame: v04's soft water suck-in), a 60 ms beat (a blue dot pulses at the centre), emerge 360 ms (ease-out-back, ~1.15 overshoot, 30 ms clockwise stagger, numbers fade in at 60 % of the way out, the outline draws in; `emerge()` fires on its first frame, 340 ms after `collapse()`: v04's six rising bubble pops). Taps are ignored during the reset (no tap-to-skip) and unlock, with the round timer, when the last tile lands. Reduced motion: a 200 ms crossfade (sound: `emerge({ gainDb: -3 })` only, no `collapse()`). Transform and opacity only (WAAPI pinned to one clock), constants in `src/motion.ts`. Sound pack v04 (`public/audio/kk_sfx.{ogg,m4a,json}`, `src/kkaudio.ts`, byte-identical to the AE's `dist/`; Opus sprite 119,801 B, under the 120 KB budget): adds `collapse`, `emerge` and `hop_0..2`; `makeTen`, `clear` and v02's take-off `hop` sprites are retired (their hooks stay as silent no-ops); `src/sfx.ts` no longer overrides any hook. `node scripts/v038.mjs [url]` checks it all, including 4x CPU (CDP) jank/long tasks and the timer (shots `shots/v038-*.png`).

- Teaching solves (`src/teach.ts`, `src/plan.ts`): with Show counting ON (before v0.3.7: on the first 3 solves of a round, a new best, a x5/x10/x20 streak, the first right answer after a mistake), and on tapping a bond in the Solved tray, the parts are counted dot by dot into the ten-frames (v0.3.3: the smaller part's light-blue dots one at a time, then the larger part's dark-blue dots carry on into the same frame's empty cells and on into the next frames, so 7 + 12 fills the first frame at 10 and 24 + 26 ends as five full frames; the answer tile, big under the frames, counts up by exactly 1 per dot; each completed frame rings green with a green ring flash on the counter, and the make-ten frame where the colours meet also shows its caption ("7 + 3 = 10", "24 + 6 = 30") while the count holds ~0.6 s; v0.3.3.1: the two parts sit as source tiles either side of the counter (light blue left, dark blue right, white numbers); every dot launches from its own tile, which drains top to bottom like a glass and pops into a coloured outline when its group is done; the tile turns green on the total; Easy 300 ms per dot, Medium 150 ms, Hard 60-72 ms), then the counter rises straight into a part-whole bond (the whole lesson is about 6–9 s by level and sum: the frames, counters and key fade out in place, the answer rises, the bond builds in 0.8 s, the caption lands (with `cheer()`; v0.3.8: no confetti burst), each number pulses in turn and the bond holds until 3.3 s, then clears). Ten-frames are always full size, up to 3 per row, and each part keeps its own colour. The timer is paused throughout. v0.3.6: no tap-to-skip; the lesson always plays in full, board taps do nothing (nothing is queued) until the next board is ready; Quit still works. `node scripts/noskip-v036.mjs [url]` checks taps during the beat, the lesson and a chip replay do nothing (nothing is replayed on the next board), input is live again at once, mute works mid-lesson and Quit mid-lesson. - Bond Lab: an untimed mode from the menu. Pick a whole from 5 to 20, split its counters (in ten-frames) with the slider, find every pair for a star (`kk.lab.v1`), then try the missing-part quiz.
- `src/puzzle.ts`: generator (unique answer, unique pair, no duplicates, every number 1..cap). Easy and Medium pick from every valid board, enumerated once per size; Hard builds boards at random. No sum repeats within a round; when a small pool runs out (Easy has 20 sums) the round cycles through it again, never repeating the last sum. `npm test` checks every level and size for the cap, exactly one solution and repeats.
