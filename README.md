# Kakooma v0.3.4.1

Maths trainer for Greg Tang's KAKOOMA puzzle. Pick a level (Easy 10, Medium 20, Hard 50: the biggest number on the board, the sum included) and a board (square, pentagon, hexagon or heptagon: 4–7 numbers; Easy offers 4 or 5, because 6 or 7 different numbers up to 10 can't make a fair board). Exactly one number is the sum of two others; tap it. 60-second rounds, streaks (Blaze grows at x5, x10 and x20), a 3-second penalty for a wrong tap, and a leaderboard per board.

Play: https://spacetourist.github.io/Kakooma/

- Teaching solves (`src/teach.ts`, `src/plan.ts`): on the first 3 solves of a round, a new best, a x5/x10/x20 streak, the first right answer after a mistake, and on tapping a bond in the Solved tray, the parts are counted dot by dot into the ten-frames (v0.3.3: the smaller part's light-blue dots one at a time, then the larger part's dark-blue dots carry on into the same frame's empty cells and on into the next frames, so 7 + 12 fills the first frame at 10 and 24 + 26 ends as five full frames; the answer tile, big under the frames, counts up by exactly 1 per dot; each completed frame rings green with a green ring flash on the counter, and the make-ten frame where the colours meet also shows its caption ("7 + 3 = 10", "24 + 6 = 30") while the count holds ~0.6 s; v0.3.3.1: the two parts sit as source tiles either side of the counter (light blue left, dark blue right, white numbers); every dot launches from its own tile, which drains top to bottom like a glass and pops into a coloured outline when its group is done; the tile turns green on the total; Easy 300 ms per dot, Medium 150 ms, Hard 60-72 ms), then the counter rises straight into a part-whole bond (the whole lesson is about 6–9 s by level and sum: the frames, counters and key fade out in place, the answer rises, the bond builds in 0.8 s, the caption lands with one confetti burst, each number pulses in turn and the bond holds until 3.3 s, then clears). Ten-frames are always full size, up to 3 per row, and each part keeps its own colour. The timer is paused throughout; tap anywhere to skip. Other solves get a quick bounce. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Bond Lab: an untimed mode from the menu. Pick a whole from 5 to 20, split its counters (in ten-frames) with the slider, find every pair for a star (`kk.lab.v1`), then try the missing-part quiz.
- `src/puzzle.ts`: generator (unique answer, unique pair, no duplicates, every number 1..cap). Easy and Medium pick from every valid board, enumerated once per size; Hard builds boards at random. No sum repeats within a round; when a small pool runs out (Easy has 20 sums) the round cycles through it again, never repeating the last sum. `npm test` checks every level and size for the cap, exactly one solution and repeats.
- Levels (v0.3): the last choice is saved (`kk.lv`); new players start on Easy, returning v0.2 players on Hard. The leaderboard is per level and size (`kk.board.v2`); v0.2 scores (`kk.board.v1`, no cap, largest tile 40–58) were copied once to Hard and v1 is left in place.
- Art: GD clean v3 (`design/kakooma/STYLE.md`). Tokens are CSS variables at the top of `src/style.css`; Blaze is `public/assets/img/blaze.png`. Fonts: Fredoka and Nunito, self-hosted under OFL (`public/licenses`).
- Leaderboard: `localStorage` (`kk.board.v2`), top 10 per level and board, on this device only.
- Results (v0.3.1): AVG SOLVE is the mean time from a board appearing to its correct tap (`src/stats.ts`). A new #1 for that level and board gets gold score/rank cards and Blaze hopping in sunglasses above RETRY (newBest sounds at that reveal too, never twice within 1.5 s); otherwise Blaze just bobs.
- Sound (v0.3.4): the Audio Engineer's pack v02c is wired in: `public/audio/kk_sfx.ogg` (Opus, 111 KB) with `kk_sfx.m4a` (AAC) as the Safari/iOS path and the automatic fallback if Opus fails to decode, `kk_sfx.json` (sprite map), `src/kkaudio.ts` (the module) and `public/audio/LICENSES.md` (all sources CC0). `src/sfx.ts` re-exports the hooks (plus `total`, `stopAll`, mute) and loads the sprite from the Vite base (`/Kakooma/audio/`) after the page's load event. Nothing plays before the first tap/key (the pack unlocks the AudioContext on that gesture); the context is suspended while the tab is hidden; tap-to-skip calls `stopAll()`. The round speaker button on the menu (left of Blaze) mutes: master gain to 0, queued sounds cleared, every later hook ignored; saved in `localStorage` (`kk.mute`); turning sound back on plays `uiTap`. `node scripts/audio-v034.mjs [url]` checks unlock, Opus/AAC, the 200s, skip, mute and console errors; `node scripts/loadtime.mjs <url>` measures first load.
- Testing hooks: `?level=easy|medium|hard` sets the level without saving it; `?round=20` shortens a round; `?teach=quick|full` forces the solve style; `?reduced` simulates reduced motion. Sound hooks in `src/sfx.ts` are no-ops whose API matches the audio pack (`kkaudio.ts`), not wired in yet; `scripts/sfx-hooks.mjs` checks when each fires. `node scripts/count-v033.mjs [url]` (count + in-count make-ten per level: +1 per dot, continuous fill, key, pace, hook timings; `count-v032.mjs` is the v0.3.2 version), `node scripts/e2e-v031.mjs [url]` (results screen at 360/390/412 px), `node scripts/e2e-v030.mjs [url]` (levels, 390 px shots), `node scripts/e2e.mjs [url]` `node scripts/e2e-v011.mjs [url]` and `node scripts/e2e-v021.mjs [url]` run (plus `scripts/solve-time.mjs [url]` for the teaching-solve length `scripts/handover-v022.mjs [url]` for the frame-by-frame handover check, and `scripts/specks-v0231.mjs [url]` + `python3 scripts/specks-pixels.py shots/v0231-specks.json` for the pixel check that no bond-line end-caps show before the branches grow) the headless play-throughs (need playwright-core in /tmp/pw).
