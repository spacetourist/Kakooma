# Kakooma v0.3.6

Maths trainer for Greg Tang's KAKOOMA puzzle. Pick a level (Easy 10, Medium 20, Hard 50: the biggest number on the board, the sum included) and a board (square, pentagon, hexagon or heptagon: 4–7 numbers; Easy offers 4 or 5, because 6 or 7 different numbers up to 10 can't make a fair board). Exactly one number is the sum of two others; tap it. 60-second rounds, streaks (Blaze grows at x5, x10 and x20), a 3-second penalty for a wrong tap, and a leaderboard per board.

Play: https://spacetourist.github.io/Kakooma/

- Teaching solves (`src/teach.ts`, `src/plan.ts`): on the first 3 solves of a round, a new best, a x5/x10/x20 streak, the first right answer after a mistake, and on tapping a bond in the Solved tray, the parts are counted dot by dot into the ten-frames (v0.3.3: the smaller part's light-blue dots one at a time, then the larger part's dark-blue dots carry on into the same frame's empty cells and on into the next frames, so 7 + 12 fills the first frame at 10 and 24 + 26 ends as five full frames; the answer tile, big under the frames, counts up by exactly 1 per dot; each completed frame rings green with a green ring flash on the counter, and the make-ten frame where the colours meet also shows its caption ("7 + 3 = 10", "24 + 6 = 30") while the count holds ~0.6 s; v0.3.3.1: the two parts sit as source tiles either side of the counter (light blue left, dark blue right, white numbers); every dot launches from its own tile, which drains top to bottom like a glass and pops into a coloured outline when its group is done; the tile turns green on the total; Easy 300 ms per dot, Medium 150 ms, Hard 60-72 ms), then the counter rises straight into a part-whole bond (the whole lesson is about 6–9 s by level and sum: the frames, counters and key fade out in place, the answer rises, the bond builds in 0.8 s, the caption lands with one confetti burst, each number pulses in turn and the bond holds until 3.3 s, then clears). Ten-frames are always full size, up to 3 per row, and each part keeps its own colour. The timer is paused throughout. v0.3.6: no tap-to-skip; the lesson always plays in full, board taps do nothing (nothing is queued) until the next board is ready; Quit still works. `node scripts/noskip-v036.mjs [url]` checks taps during the beat, the lesson and a chip replay do nothing (nothing is replayed on the next board), input is live again at once, mute works mid-lesson and Quit mid-lesson. Other solves get a quick bounce. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Bond Lab: an untimed mode from the menu. Pick a whole from 5 to 20, split its counters (in ten-frames) with the slider, find every pair for a star (`kk.lab.v1`), then try the missing-part quiz.
- `src/puzzle.ts`: generator (unique answer, unique pair, no duplicates, every number 1..cap). Easy and Medium pick from every valid board, enumerated once per size; Hard builds boards at random. No sum repeats within a round; when a small pool runs out (Easy has 20 sums) the round cycles through it again, never repeating the last sum. `npm test` checks every level and size for the cap, exactly one solution and repeats.
- Levels (v0.3): the last choice is saved (`kk.lv`); new players start on Easy, returning v0.2 players on Hard. The leaderboard is per level and size (`kk.board.v2`); v0.2 scores (`kk.board.v1`, no cap, largest tile 40–58) were copied once to Hard and v1 is left in place.
- Art: GD clean v3 (`design/kakooma/STYLE.md`). Tokens are CSS variables at the top of `src/style.css`; Blaze is `public/assets/img/blaze.png`. Fonts: Fredoka and Nunito, self-hosted under OFL (`public/licenses`).
- Leaderboard: `localStorage` (`kk.board.v2`), top 10 per level and board, on this device only.
- Results (v0.3.1): AVG SOLVE is the mean time from a board appearing to its correct tap (`src/stats.ts`). A new #1 for that level and board gets gold score/rank cards and Blaze hopping in sunglasses above RETRY (newBest sounds at that reveal too, never twice within 1.5 s); otherwise Blaze just bobs.
- Sound (v0.3.4): the Audio Engineer's pack is wired in (v03 since v0.3.6: v02c + `blaze(pose)` and `link()`, 64 sprites): `public/audio/kk_sfx.ogg` (Opus, 118 KB) with `kk_sfx.m4a` (AAC) as the Safari/iOS path and the automatic fallback if Opus fails to decode, `kk_sfx.json` (sprite map), `src/kkaudio.ts` (the module) and `public/audio/LICENSES.md` (all sources CC0). `src/sfx.ts` re-exports the hooks (plus `total`, `stopAll`, mute) and loads the sprite from the Vite base (`/Kakooma/audio/`) after the page's load event. Nothing plays before the first tap/key (the pack unlocks the AudioContext on that gesture); the context is suspended while the tab is hidden; quit / round end call `stopAll()`. The round speaker button on the menu (left of Blaze) mutes: master gain to 0, queued sounds cleared, every later hook ignored; saved in `localStorage` (`kk.mute`); turning sound back on plays `uiTap`. `node scripts/audio-v034.mjs [url]` checks unlock, Opus/AAC, the 200s, stopAll on quit, mute and console errors; `node scripts/loadtime.mjs <url>` measures first load. `node scripts/audio-v036.mjs [url]` checks the v03 hooks with real playback (each source matched to its sprite): link with the lines, blaze 100 ms after the pose frame (think 0), sprite keys, mute.
- Connect beat (v0.3.5, GD): every correct pick, every level, timer paused: 0–0.15 s the answer pops green to 1.08 and every other tile dims to 40 %; 0.1–0.5 s the two parts light up (#9cc0ff smaller, #3a7bff larger) and a thick round-ended line in each part's colour draws into the answer, both arriving together (`sfx.link()` at 0.1 s, the AE's v03 follow-through; no partPop in the reveal). v0.3.6 (GD): the lines sit on a layer under all the number tiles and run centre to centre, so both ends are tucked under the circles and only the stretch between the tiles shows (the visible stretch keeps the 0.1–0.5 s draw); dimmed tiles keep an opaque disc under them so a line never shows through; 0.5–0.7 s one soft pulse on the answer and the sum strip fills in; hold, then the lines and dimming fade over 0.15 s and the counting lesson or the next puzzle takes over at 1.0 s. Reduced motion: the lines simply appear. It always plays in full (v0.3.6: no tap-to-skip; taps during it do nothing). Replaces the 0.5 s quick bounce. `node scripts/connect-v035.mjs [url]` (frozen-frame shots `shots/v035-connect-*.png`, 50 ms sequences in `shots/v035-seq/`).
- Confetti (v0.3.5.1, GD spec): canvas-confetti (lazy chunk, ~4.3 KB gzipped, fetched after load), one shared full-screen instance drawn in a web worker (taps never stutter). Colours #3a7bff, #9cc0ff, #22c55e, #ffc928, #ff8a1f; ~50 % circles, 30 % squares, 20 % stars (star built once with shapeFromPath). Normal solve: as the bond completes, two bursts of 40 from 30 px either side of the bond centre, angled in at 60°/120° (spread 55, velocity 32, gravity 0.9, decay 0.92, ticks 140, scalar 0.9). Big moments (streak 3/5/10, a new #1): a 1.2 s shower of 6 × 25 from the top edge, 200 ms apart, x spread evenly over 0.1–0.9 with jitter, stars 50 %; with a lesson it replaces the bond burst inside the lesson, without one the solved board (sum still up) holds until 0.3 s after the last burst (~2.0 s) so it never lands on a playable board. At most ~150 pieces alive; a burst < 300 ms after the last is skipped. Off for reduced motion. `node scripts/v0351.mjs [url] [beforeUrl]`.
- Confetti (v0.3.5): canvas-confetti (lazy chunk, ~4.3 KB gzipped, fetched after load; drawn in a web worker on one fixed canvas so taps never stutter). Circles, rounded squares and small stars in #3a7bff, #9cc0ff, green, #ffc928 and orange. The usual burst is two angled bursts (~45 pieces each) from either side of the finished bond, with drift and 3D flutter/tumble; streaks of 3/5/10 and a new #1 also get a star shower from the top. Off for reduced motion.
- Blaze reactions (v0.3.6, `src/blaze.ts`): GD's v2 poses (`public/assets/img/blaze/*.webp`, 172×158 from 634×581 masters framed exactly like `blaze.png`, no strip, no scaling/offset, 2x the 86 px display, ~6–7 KB each, preloaded in idle time after first paint and load) swap in over `blaze.png` inside `#blazeBody` for 0.9 s with a squash-and-stretch on the existing WAAPI transform; reduced motion swaps the picture only. Comeback (first correct pick after a miss): thumbs + beaming bounce, always. Streak 3/5/10: cheer + taller flare + 4/5/7 sparks, always, on the star shower's first burst together with `streak(n)` (lesson and held board; quit / round end drop it; no shower, e.g. reduced motion: at the tap) (replaces the x5/x10 tier hop). Wrong pick: oops (v2: sheepish grin) + kind wobble on every other wrong pick (the other ones keep the head tilt). Count reaches 10 in a lesson (green flash): clap. No tap on the board for 8 s in play: think, held until the next tap. Speedy solve (board answered in under 2 s): whoa + flare, at most once a round (`WHOA_ON`, on since v2). oops/clap/think/whoa: at most one every 3 solves; otherwise Blaze keeps his normal hop/tilt. One sound hook `sfx.blaze(pose)` fires on the swap frame; the v03 pack plays it 100 ms later (`BLAZE_OFFSET`, think 0). One reaction per solve (a milestone's cheer wins over whoa). `?blaze=<pose>` holds a pose for review (triggers off); `node scripts/blaze-v035.mjs [url]` checks it all (shots `shots/v035-*.png`); `node scripts/blaze-align-v2.mjs` checks every pose against idle (no shrink/drop; shots `shots/v2-*.png`).
- Streak sound (v0.3.5.2, AE): when a streak level gets the star shower (x5, x10), `streak(n)` fires in the same tick as the shower's first burst (in the lesson at the bond, or at 0.5 s on the held board when there's no lesson); `correct()` stays on the tap. x20 and reduced motion (no shower) keep `streak(n)` on the tap. Quit / round end before the shower drop it. `node scripts/streak-v0352.mjs [url]` logs the timings.
